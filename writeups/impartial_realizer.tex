\title{The Impartial Realizer Theorem:\\
A Pass-Free Quadratic Arena}
\author{a9lim}

\begin{abstract}
The weighted-source \textsc{witt-fifo} theorem of the companion flagship
paper realizes $\{Q=0\}$ as a normal-play $P$-set for every quadratic
refinement $Q$ of every alternating form on $\F_2^n$, under the optimal
$(w_0,c)=(1,1)$ access contract.  We prove that the arena can be made
impartial.  Delete PASS and allow a ko-protected FIFO front to close once
the untouched set is empty.  Ko still forces every matching edge to overlap,
while the core now has exactly two moves per loaded coin.  One seat-symmetric
tail move at charge one therefore compiles the forced charge to ordinary
normal play: the first player forces the tail when $Q(x)=1$, and the second
player forces its absence when $Q(x)=0$.  The construction is uniform over
all finite dimensions, alternating forms, refinements, and inputs, and uses
at most one singleton query per transition.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement}

Throughout, $V=\F_2^n$, $B$ is an alternating bilinear form on $V$, and
$Q$ is a quadratic refinement of $B$, i.e.\ $Q(u+v)=Q(u)+Q(v)+B(u,v)$
with $Q(0)=0$.  The access contract F1--F3/N1--N2, the loading map, and
the realization theorem are those of the flagship
paper~\cite{goldarf}: statics are refinement-blind (F1), each move
predicate and transition observes $Q$ through at most $c$ oracle queries
at points of Hamming weight at most $w_0$, with $(w_0,c)$ independent of
dimension (F2), the semantics is one declared outcome convention (F3),
and exactness holds uniformly over the full torsor of refinements of
every form in the family (N1) within the stated budget (N2).

\begin{theorem}[impartial uniform realizer]\label{thm:impartial}
\PROVED\ There is one impartial normal-play rule satisfying
F1--F3/N1--N2 at $(w_0,c)=(1,1)$ whose loaded root at $x$ is $P$ exactly
when $Q(x)=0$, uniformly over every quadratic refinement $Q$ of every
alternating form $B$ in every finite dimension.
\end{theorem}

\section{The proved fence}

Three flagship results show what the theorem must accomplish.

\begin{theorem}[coin-turning linear ceiling]\label{thm:ceiling}
\PROVED~\cite[Thm.~A]{goldarf}
Every Winning Ways coin-turning game has configuration $P$-set equal to
the kernel of an $\F_2$-linear nimber-valued map; no coin-turning $P$-set
is a quadric of positive polar rank.
\end{theorem}

An impartial realizer therefore cannot be a disjunctive sum of
coin-turning heaps, and more generally cannot present its positions as
configurations whose Grundy value is XOR-additive over independent
components.  Whatever forces the quadric must entangle the coordinates
dynamically, as the FIFO queue does.

\begin{theorem}[abelian obstruction]\label{thm:abelian}
\PROVED~\cite[Lem.~abelian, Thm.~B]{goldarf}
In any commutative monoid, squaring is an endomorphism, so any quadratic
datum read through homomorphic coordinates has polarization identically
zero.  In particular no commutative game-value monoid -- Sprague--Grundy
values under disjunctive sum, mis\`ere quotients, turn-parity counters --
carries a quadratic refinement with $B\neq0$.
\end{theorem}

This does \emph{not} exclude the theorem.  It excludes
factorizations: an impartial candidate cannot read $Q$ off any
commutative value quotient of its positions.  The refinement must enter
intensionally, through the move relation.  Impartial play retains the one
asymmetry the flagship identifies as structurally
available~\cite[Rem.~screen]{goldarf} -- the first-/second-player
asymmetry of whose turn it is -- even though its move \emph{sets} are
equal.  Normal play is precisely the reading of that asymmetry.

\begin{theorem}[transcript-span bound]\label{thm:span}
\PROVED~\cite[transcript-span theorem]{goldarf}
Any transcript-stable exact rule observes refinement values at directions
spanning the input; in the original frame, weight-$\leq w$ observations
number at least $\wt(x)/w$.
\end{theorem}

The bound is seat-blind: an impartial realizer at $w_0=1$ still needs the
full singleton support $\{e_j : x_j=1\}$.  No information discount is
available for symmetry.

\section{The pass-free rule}

Use the flagship's deterministic $B$-only adapted basis $f_i$, active
coordinates $y_i$, public corrections $p_i=P_B(f_i)$, and source pair
$S_j^0,S_j^1$ for every $x_j=1$.  Let $G_0$ be the public potential
matching consisting of every loaded Witt edge and every loaded source pair,
regardless of the source weight $q_j=Q(e_j)$.

The core state is $(U,L,\mathrm{ko},\sigma)$, with untouched coins $U$, FIFO
queue $L$, and accumulated charge $\sigma$.  OPEN is unchanged.  Delete
PASS, and declare CLOSE legal exactly when $L\ne\varnothing$ and
\[
  \mathrm{ko}=0\quad\text{or}\quad U=\varnothing.
\]
At a drained state there is one move to a fixed optionless sink exactly when
$\sigma=1$.  Every move is seat-symmetric.

\section{Proof}

Fix either designated seat.  Its public policy is deterministic: while
$U\ne\varnothing$, never CLOSE; from an empty queue OPEN the least untouched
coin; otherwise OPEN the untouched $G_0$-mate of the front if it exists, and
OPEN the least untouched coin if it does not.  Once $U=\varnothing$, CLOSE.

Call a checkpoint handed to the opponent safe when every legal CLOSE has
zero live $G_0$-degree.  An OPEN onto an empty queue sets ko.  If untouched
coins remain, CLOSE is illegal; if none remain, every live degree is zero.
From a nonempty queue, opening the front's public mate removes its unique
possible live neighbour, while opening any coin when no mate remains
preserves degree zero.  Thus every policy move hands over a safe checkpoint.
An opponent OPEN returns the turn; an opponent CLOSE has zero live degree
and returns the turn.  The newly legal ko-CLOSE is harmless because its
legality forces $U=\varnothing$.  Induction on $2|U|+|L|$ proves that every
CLOSE along a policy-consistent play has zero public live degree.  The empty
root is safe for the second seat, so the proof works for either seat.

Every public pair overlaps.  Otherwise the earlier-opened endpoint would
CLOSE while its mate remained untouched, giving live degree one in the
matching $G_0$.  Each pair is therefore charged exactly once.  Hence
\[
 \sigma=\sum_i y_ip_i+\sum_h y_{a_h}y_{b_h}
       +\sum_{j:x_j=1}q_j=Q(x)
\]
by the adapted-basis expansion.

The exact clock $2|U|+|L|$ drops by one on every OPEN and CLOSE.  A
nonterminal core never stalls: OPEN exists when $U\ne\varnothing$, and the
exhausted-board CLOSE exists when $U=\varnothing\ne L$.  Thus a board with
$N$ coins has exactly $2N$ core moves, after which it is the first player's
turn.  If $Q(x)=1$, the first player follows the policy, forces
$\sigma=1$, and takes the tail.  If $Q(x)=0$, the second player follows the
same policy, forces $\sigma=0$, and leaves the first player without a move.
This proves Theorem~\ref{thm:impartial}.  The statement is strategic: it
does not assert that arbitrary off-policy plays have charge $Q(x)$.

For F1, take the refinement-blind position universe to contain all coherent
core states for both charge bits plus the sink; the fixed terminal set is
the sink together with drained charge-zero states.  Only a source OPEN whose
mate is queued queries $Q(e_j)$, once and at weight one, proving F2/N2 at
$(1,1)$.  Ordinary normal play is F3, and the proof is uniform over the
full refinement torsor, giving N1.  The empty input is already an optionless
$P$-position because $Q(0)=0$.

\begin{remark}[contract-only shortcut]
The written per-transition contract alone also admits a forced scan path:
query the active singletons one per move, accumulate
$P_B(x)+\sum_{j:x_j=1}Q(e_j)=Q(x)$, and parity-correct the path at the end.
This meets the observation lower bound but is a distributed evaluator.  The
pass-free FIFO theorem is the stronger intended result: one public policy
forces play-dependent overlap interactions for the entire refinement
torsor.  Thus access metering controls information but does not by itself
axiomatize non-evaluative structure.
\end{remark}

The pass-free transition, exact clock, safe-front strategy semantics, and
impartial tail compiler are kernel-checked without axioms in
\path{formal/Ogdoad/ImpartialRealizer.lean}.  The weighted source loading and
adapted-basis composition remain the explicit paper-level synthesis.  The
complete theorem has been folded into the flagship paper~\cite{goldarf}.
