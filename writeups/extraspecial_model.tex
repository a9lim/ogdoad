\begin{abstract}
The flagship paper's extraspecial dictionary identifies quadratic
refinements $Q$ of alternating forms $B$ on $V=\F_2^n$ with central
extensions $1\to\mathbb{Z}/2\to E_Q\to V\to1$: squares in $E_Q$ read the
diagonal, commutators read the polar form.  Its equivariance screen shows
that once the extension is available as an object, the realizing rule
layer is canonical -- the squaring rule is the extraspecial squaring map
read as a one-move relation -- and its no-go theorems show the screen
cannot be met by commutative value-worlds, by symmetry classes beyond the
orthogonal group, or by $E$-translation dynamics.  The flagship's fourth
residual open question is therefore structural: exhibit a
\emph{game-native} model of $E_{Q_a}$ -- positions built from game
values, multiplication from game constructions -- producing the Gold
diagonal $q_i=Q_a(e_i)$ as squares, without an evaluation circuit for
$Q_a$.  A positive answer upgrades the flagship's covariant realization
to a structural one.  This note fixes the demands such a model must
meet, collects the proved fence, and records candidate directions: the
mover-bit asymmetry as the central involution, noncommutative game
compounds (ordinal sum and sequential join) as multiplication carriers,
and the Brown-selector precedent that partizan outcome classes can host
central data.
\end{abstract}

\section{The dictionary and the problem}

Throughout, $V=\F_2^n$, $B$ alternating, $Q$ a quadratic refinement of
$B$, and $Q_a(x)=\Tr(x\,x^{2^a})$ the Gold trace family on nimber fields
that motivates everything~\cite{goldarf}.

\begin{theorem}[extraspecial dictionary]\label{thm:dict}
\STANDARD~(proved self-contained in \cite{goldarf}; classical:
\cite{Quillen71,Gorenstein80})
Central extensions $1\to Z\to E\to V\to1$ with $Z=\{1,z\}\cong\mathbb{Z}/2$
correspond bijectively to quadratic maps $Q\colon V\to\F_2$: lifted
squares are central and well defined, giving $\tilde x^2=z^{Q(x)}$, and
lifted commutators give $[\tilde x,\tilde y]=z^{B(x,y)}$ with $B$ the
polar form of $Q$.  $E_Q$ is abelian iff $B=0$, and extraspecial iff $B$
is nondegenerate.
\end{theorem}

Two structural consequences fix what any model must contain.

\begin{lemma}[demands]\label{lem:demands}
\STANDARD\
Any model $(N,Z,\pi)$ of $E_Q$ -- a monoid $N$ with central two-element
subgroup $Z$ and surjection $\pi\colon N\to V$ with fibers the
$Z$-cosets -- satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item if $B\neq0$, $N$ is noncommutative;
\item if $Q(v)=1$ for some $v$, any lift $\tilde v$ has order four, so
$N$ contains $4$-torsion.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
(i) is the dictionary: commutators read $B$.  (ii): $\tilde v^2=z\neq1$
and $\tilde v^4=z^2=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{question}[game-native extraspecial model]\label{q:model}
\OPEN~(the flagship's stated open question)
Does any game-native construction realize $E_{Q_a}$ -- positions built
from game values, multiplication from game constructions -- equivalently,
produce the diagonal data $q_i=Q_a(e_i)$ as squares in a noncommutative
game-built structure, without an evaluation circuit for $Q_a$?
\end{question}

The payoff is canonicity: over the abstract group the flagship's
equivariance screen is already satisfiable by the squaring rule, which
consults only the multiplication of $E$.  A game-native $E_{Q_a}$ would
therefore upgrade the weighted-source realization, which is covariant
(public preprocessing transported along the orthogonal group), to a
structural one, where the arena \emph{is} the extension.

\section{The proved fence}

\begin{theorem}[commutative squaring collapse]\label{thm:abelian}
\PROVED~\cite[Lem.~abelian, Thm.~B]{goldarf}
In any commutative monoid, squaring is an endomorphism, so any model as
in Lemma~\ref{lem:demands} inside a commutative monoid has linear
squaring form: $B\equiv0$.  No commutative game-value monoid --
Sprague--Grundy values under disjunctive sum, mis\`ere quotients,
turn-parity counters, coin-turning configuration sums -- hosts $E_Q$
once $B\neq0$.
\end{theorem}

In concrete game terms the two demands of Lemma~\ref{lem:demands} fail
separately for the default operations: disjunctive sum of impartial
games is commutative \emph{and} elementary abelian ($G+G$ is a
$P$-position for every impartial $G$), so it lacks both the
noncommutativity and the $4$-torsion.  The model needs a genuinely
different multiplication.

\begin{theorem}[the symmetry dial has no middle]\label{thm:E}
\PROVED~\cite[Thm.~E]{goldarf}
By Dye--Dieudonn\'e maximality of $\Orth(Q)$ in $\Sp(B)$ and the Witt
orbital classification, a rule invariant under any group strictly larger
than $\Orth(Q)$ has dead $P$-set, while a fully $\Orth(Q)$- or
$\Aut(E)$-equivariant rule factors extensionally through pointwise $Q$-
and $B$-evaluation.  $E$-naturality must be covariance, not invariance.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[$E$-translation no-go]\label{thm:H}
\PROVED~\cite[Thm.~H]{goldarf}
For polar rank $\geq4$, no left-$E$-equivariant rule on $E_Q$ with moves
$g\to gn$ for a fixed nonempty move set $N\subseteq E_Q$ has $P$-set the
involution locus $\pi^{-1}(\{Q=0\})$.
\end{theorem}

So the model cannot be reached by making play \emph{more} symmetric: the
extension must be built, not imposed, and once built its group structure
must drive the dynamics covariantly.  The search space is exactly the
noncommutative resources games natively possess.

\begin{remark}[adjacent negative: the game-exterior collapse]\label{rem:exterior}
\PROVED~\cite[game-exterior appendix]{goldarf}
For ambient-coherent coefficient-faithful Clifford data on the additive
group of short partizan games, two-divisibility forces all quadratic and
polar values into $\bigcap_k4^kR$, hence zero over $\mathbb{Z}$, in
characteristic $2$, and over $\mathbb{Z}/4$.  This kills the
\emph{additive, algebra-valued} route: one cannot source the extension's
quadratic data from a Clifford structure carried by the full game group.
The extension target is group-theoretic rather than algebra-theoretic,
so the collapse does not decide Question~\ref{q:model}; it does warn
that ambient coherence over the whole game group is fatal, and that a
model should be sought over bounded-birthday or otherwise restricted
position sets.
\end{remark}

\section{Resources and candidate directions}

\begin{remark}[the available asymmetry]\label{rem:asymmetry}
The flagship identifies the one asymmetry that normal, mis\`ere, and
partizan play all possess and that the symmetric polar form discards:
the first-/second-player asymmetry of the move
relation~\cite[equivariance-screen remark]{goldarf}.  The central
involution $z$ is the natural tenant: a tempo token whose consumption
commutes with everything (centrality) but whose \emph{presence} records
one bit.  Under this reading, lifted noncommutativity
$[\tilde x,\tilde y]=z^{B(x,y)}$ says that interchanging the order of
two position-building operations costs a tempo exactly when their polar
pairing is on -- a move-order holonomy.  The FIFO charge mechanism of
the flagship realization is precisely such a holonomy read
\emph{covariantly}; the model question asks for it \emph{structurally}.
\SPECULATION
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[noncommutative game compounds]\label{rem:compounds}
\SPECULATION\
Theorem~\ref{thm:abelian} bars commutative operations, not game
constructions as such.  Games natively carry noncommutative compounds:
ordinal sum $G:H$ (moves in $H$ vanish when $G$ is moved), sequential
join, and side-conditional compounds.  None is a group operation on
values, so a model would quotient a compound-generated position monoid
by an indistinguishability relation tuned so that the quotient is a
finite $2$-group with the right center -- the analogue, one level up, of
mis\`ere quotient construction, which Theorem~\ref{thm:abelian} shows
must \emph{not} collapse the compound to a commutative value world.
The first concrete probe: compute indistinguishability quotients of
small ordinal-sum position algebras over the anisotropic plane
($n=2$, $q_1=q_2=b_{12}=1$, $E_Q=Q_8$) and check which quotients contain
$Q_8$; the quaternion target makes the search finite and sharp.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[the Brown-selector precedent]\label{rem:brown}
The flagship's Brown-selector theorem reads the $\mathbb{Z}/4$-valued
refinement off four partizan outcome classes
$\mathcal{N},\mathcal{R},\mathcal{P},\mathcal{L}$ with no external
product or terminal evaluator~\cite[Brown-selector theorem]{goldarf}.
This is evidence that partizan outcome observation has the capacity to
host exactly the kind of central, order-four data the extension carries
(Lemma~\ref{lem:demands}(ii)): the selector's outcome lattice is a
$\mathbb{Z}/4$ readout obtained from game structure alone.  What the
selector does not provide is multiplication -- it reads residues, it
does not compose them.  A model would need the composition law; the
selector suggests where its values can live.  \SPECULATION
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[implemented infrastructure]\label{rem:infra}
The repository implements the finite Heisenberg/Pauli representation of
the char-$2$ extraspecial surface over its $\F_2$ bitmask boundary
(\path{src/forms/char2/extraspecial.rs}): $E_Q$ as explicit matrices,
projective symplectic-transvection intertwiners, and the squaring/
commutator readouts.  This is experiment infrastructure -- a concrete
target to compare candidate game quotients against -- not a game-native
model.
\end{remark}

\section{Target}

Exhibit a set of positions generated by game constructions, a
game-built composition law on them, a central two-element subgroup, and
a surjection to the coordinate space, such that the squaring form is
$Q_a$ -- with the diagonal produced by the structure, not queried -- or
prove a further no-go narrowing ``game-native'' (for instance: no
finitely presented quotient of an ordinal-sum position monoid on
game-value generators contains $Q_8$ compatibly with its Grundy
projection).  By Theorem~\ref{thm:abelian} the source cannot be any
commutative game-value monoid; by Lemma~\ref{lem:demands} it must carry
$4$-torsion; by Theorems~\ref{thm:E} and~\ref{thm:H} neither extra
symmetry nor translation dynamics can substitute for the construction.

This note is the authoritative statement of the problem and will be
folded into the flagship paper when resolved.
